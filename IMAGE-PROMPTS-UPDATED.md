# Image Generation Prompts — Mumbai Vada Pav (UPDATED)

## Style Guide (Apply to ALL prompts)

**Consistent style anchor:**
- Shot on 35mm film, Kodak Portra 400 color tones
- Warm golden hour / tungsten bulb lighting
- Shallow depth of field
- Professional food photography quality
- No text, no watermarks, no logos, no borders
- Hyper-realistic, not illustration

**Color palette:**
- Saffron/turmeric yellow (#E3A94A)
- Warm orange tungsten (#D97706)
- Deep Mumbai night blue (#1E3A5F)
- Newspaper cream (#F5F0E8)
- Chutney green (#2D6A4F)
- Chili red (#DC2626)

**Display:** Right column of 2-col grid. Grayscale filter applied by CSS.
**Mood:** AUTHENTIC VINTAGE. Grainy. Historical. Like a real photo from a museum archive.

**Prompt:**
```
A genuine-looking black and white photograph in the style of 
1960s Indian photojournalism (Raghu Rai / Sunil Janah era). 
Mumbai textile mill workers during afternoon lunch break. 

A group of 5-6 men in white cotton shirts and dhotis or plain 
trousers, standing in a casual circle outside a large industrial 
mill building with tall brick chimney visible behind them. They 
are eating from their hands — one man clearly holding something 
like a bread roll or vada pav, mid-bite. Another man is laughing. 
Their faces show the honest fatigue and simple joy of a lunch 
break.

ground. Heavy grain — shot on Ilford HP5 or Kodak Tri-X 400. 
Slightly uneven exposure (darker edges, brighter center) like 
a hand-developed print. The paper should feel like it was 
stored in a file for 60 years.

Composition: documentary street photography, candid, not posed. 
Shot from slightly below eye level. Wide enough to see the 
industrial building context. High contrast blacks and whites, 
crushed shadows. ABSOLUTELY NO modern elements — no phones, no 
modern clothing, no plastic.

This must look like a REAL archival photograph from 1966, not 
an AI recreation. Think National Geographic archive or Times of 
India photo morgue.
```

**Key elements:**
- BLACK AND WHITE ONLY — vintage look
- 1960s authentic documentary feel (grain, contrast, uneven exposure)
- Mill workers eating (standing, casual circle)
- Industrial chimney/building visible
- Harsh shadows (midday sun)
- Candid — NOT posed
- Must feel REAL and ARCHIVAL, not generated
